# LevelUp Rewards App Feature Report for Flyer Creation

Last reviewed: June 2026

This report summarizes the main product functions in the LevelUp Rewards app and translates them into flyer-ready marketing angles. It is based on the current app routes, product pillar notes, staff/admin tab registry, and existing marketing flyer catalog.

## Core Product Positioning

LevelUp Rewards is a school culture platform built around a simple loop:

1. Staff recognize student effort with points, coupons, quick awards, badges, raffles, and goals.
2. Students see progress immediately through the kiosk, prize shop, student portal, displays, and printed rewards.
3. Admins configure the school, manage people and rewards, and track what is happening across the building.

The strongest flyer promise is:

> Turn everyday positive behavior into visible progress, student excitement, and school-wide routines teachers can actually run.

## Main App Functions and Features

### 1. Rewards Core

Purpose:
The central points-and-prizes economy for the school.

Main functions:

- Staff award points manually or through printed coupons.
- Students redeem coupon codes from the kiosk.
- Admins define point categories, point values, rules, and reward settings.
- Students see point balances, history, eligible prizes, and redemption options.
- Prize shop supports costs, availability, stock, teacher/class visibility, pickup vouchers, and optional AI fun rewards.
- Optional vending machine support can trigger configured motor actions for physical prize dispensing.

Flyer-ready benefits:

- "Make positive behavior visible."
- "Give teachers a fast, consistent recognition routine."
- "Let students connect effort to rewards they can see."
- "Support both digital prize shop and physical pickup workflows."

Best flyer visuals:

- Student kiosk coupon redemption screen.
- Eligible prize cards.
- Teacher coupon printing or point award screen.
- Admin rewards/prize setup screen.

### 2. Student Kiosk

Purpose:
Student-facing scan-and-earn station for school devices.

Main functions:

- Students can scan an ID card, QR/barcode, prize shelf card, voucher, library item, or coupon depending on enabled settings.
- Manual code entry is available where enabled.
- Kiosk can show student points, prize eligibility, coupon redemption, raffle tickets, library checkouts, and welcome-back overlays.
- Birthday bonus, attendance sign-in, library checkout, recess checkout, prize voucher fulfillment, and coupon redemption can all connect to the kiosk experience.
- Supports student themes, animations, sounds, sponsor banners, camera scan modes, and portrait kiosk layouts.

Flyer-ready benefits:

- "A hallway-friendly station students want to use."
- "No personal phones required."
- "Scan an ID, redeem a coupon, check prizes, and keep moving."
- "Turns reward tracking into a visible school routine."

Best flyer visuals:

- Kiosk login / scan card screen.
- Coupon redemption celebration.
- Prize rail with eligible rewards.
- ID card scan flow.

### 3. Teacher and Staff Portal

Purpose:
Daily staff workspace for awarding, tracking, and running classroom routines.

Main functions:

- Welcome home with quick access to enabled tools.
- Student roster and class views.
- Coupon printing and point awards.
- Redemptions history.
- Classroom seating and quick awards.
- Reports.
- Optional add-on tabs: analytics, attendance, displays, incentives, library, achievements, badges, goals, raffle, houses, notifications, branding, integrations, student home portal, homework, and generated coupons.
- Teachers can pin feature tabs to customize the workspace.

Flyer-ready benefits:

- "Teachers get the tools they need without admin clutter."
- "Pin the features your school uses most."
- "Award points, print coupons, run raffles, and manage classroom momentum from one place."
- "Flexible enough for teachers, secretaries, prize clerks, librarians, and reports users."

Best flyer visuals:

- Teacher portal tab row.
- Coupon print flow.
- Classroom seating chart.
- Raffle or goal panel.

### 4. Admin Portal

- Print student, staff, and prize ID cards.
- Manage themes, school logo, kiosk layout, sponsor banners, language, motion, and device settings.
- Enable/disable feature tabs and product pillars.
- View analytics, reports, and activity snapshots.

Flyer-ready benefits:

- "One place to configure the whole school."
- "Turn features on as your school grows."

## Suggested Flyer Structure

For most future flyers:

1. Lead with the audience-specific promise.
2. Show one strong live app screenshot.
3. Use three feature blocks: daily workflow, student experience, admin visibility.
4. Add a small "works with" row for related tools.
5. Close with setup/procurement/contact language.

Example for a Classroom Management flyer:

- Headline: "Tap the seating chart. Reward the room."
- Screenshot: classroom seating grid.
- Feature blocks:
  - Quick awards from every desk.
  - Behavior notes and bathroom pass visibility.
  - Fullscreen class display for live progress.
- Works with: Rewards, Attendance, Reports, Parent Portal, Displays.
- CTA: "Bring classroom routines into the LevelUp culture loop."
