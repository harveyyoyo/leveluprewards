The Challenge & The Solution

The Challenge: Managing student motivation, positive behavior incentives, and school culture milestones manually is exhausting for your staff. Counting physical tickets, tracking spreadsheets, and managing prize inventories eats up hours of instructional time.
The Solution: The LevelUp Rewards Kiosk is a plug-and-play physical hardware station paired with a powerful cloud-based backend. Students autonomously scan their custom barcodes to track achievement points, view reward tier eligibility, and redeem incentives effortlessly.

Federal funding paths

  • Option 1: Federal Title IV, Part A Funds (SSAE)
    Meets Criteria: Safe & Healthy Students (PBIS) & Effective Use of Technology (Integrated Hardware & Cloud).
    Justification: Procuring touchscreens and automated behavioral technology to actively boost building-wide culture metrics.
    NYC Process: Publics allocate inside FAMIS under Code VS00109014. Yeshivas/Privates request their BNPSS coordinator map this vendor.
  • Option 2: Federal Title I, Part A Funds (Engagement)
    Meets Criteria: Targets student motivation, school climate improvements, and closing achievement gaps.
    Justification: Utilizing automated PBIS systems to incentivize attendance, effort, and positive citizenship among at-risk cohorts.
    NYC Process: Switch the funding source in FAMIS to Title I and enter standard quick-codes.

🏛️ State Claims & Discretionary Options

  • Option 3: NYS Nonpublic School Safety Equipment (NPSE) Grant
    Pillar: Security, checkpoints, and student management technology.
    Justification: Entrance checkpoints and touch kiosks function as a robust student accountability and campus movement tracking system.
    Process: Review general supply or safety-equipment purchase paths with your business office before submitting any reimbursement claim.
  • Option 4: NYS Mandated Services Reimbursement (MSR)
    Pillar: Covering administrative costs of mandated pupil tracking.
    Justification: Automated arrival scans, barcode logging, and pupil reporting sheets map directly under Mandated Service #1: Pupil Attendance Report (PAR).
    Process: Discuss whether ongoing software licensing can align with annual MSR documentation requirements.
  • Option 5: Discretionary Tax Levy & Technology Fees
    Pillar: Open, frictionless purchasing with zero administrative hurdles.
    Process: Direct open-market PO approval by the principal utilizing general supplies or student tech fee reserves.

⚠️ Pro-Tip for Immediate Purchase

Keep your initial pilot or hardware system order quote under $20,000. This may simplify review, but your business office should confirm the current purchasing threshold, approval path, and documentation needed.

📝 FAMIS Open-Market Ordering Steps

  1. 1 · Log In: Log directly into the DOE central FAMIS Portal.
  2. 2 · Create PO: Start a new Open-Market Purchase Order.
  3. 3 · Vendor Code: Enter NYC Vendor Code VS00109014 (LevelUp EdTech will pop up instantly).
  4. 4 · Object Codes: Use 0337 for cloud software licensing and 0338 / 0300 for physical equipment.
  5. 5 · Line Items: Manually enter the line items from your quote.
